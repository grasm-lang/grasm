Status

Every number here is written by the run that proved it — scripts/allgates.sh records them after every gate passes, and the page is regenerated from that record. None of them is typed by hand.

What holds

362programs, three engines
189identical in wasm
227cross-compiled, agreeing
0open defects
67findings recorded
14generators

Interpreter, bundle and native builds agree byte for byte on stdout, exit code and diagnostics. A wasm build agrees on the 297 programs that do not need a filesystem, a socket, a process or threads. The same 362 cross-compile to x86_64 and agree there. Every generated program is checked for what it keeps as well as for what it prints.

What is not claimed

Grasm is not faster than Rust, Go or Zig, and no benchmark here says it is. It is not battle-tested. Defects are still being found at roughly one per working session — falling, but not zero, and a language with zero known defects and no users has told you more about its test suite than about itself.

How that is kept honest

All 97 findings are written down in ProjectGold/FINDINGS.md, including the ones that were the test harness's fault, the ones withdrawn as wrong, and the ones caught before they shipped. Every gate is verified by breaking the thing it guards and confirming it fails.
