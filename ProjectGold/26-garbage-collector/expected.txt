    mark-sweep left: [kept, root]
--- the summary ---
Reference counting freed everything mark-and-sweep did, except a cycle.
That is the whole difference between the two algorithms, and it is why
Grasm's own `ref` is generational rather than counted: a generation
check catches a stale handle without needing to know whether anything
still points at the slot ([T-REF-GENERATIONAL]).
